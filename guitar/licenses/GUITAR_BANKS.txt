Included instruments: acoustic_guitar_nylon, acoustic_guitar_steel,
electric_guitar_jazz, electric_guitar_clean, overdriven_guitar.
Changes: selected 25 notes per instrument (MIDI 40–88 in whole-tone steps),
retained their original MP3 bytes, and repackaged them as embedded data.
Playback applies amplitude, velocity-dependent filtering, envelopes,
pitch interpolation and the user's effect settings.
