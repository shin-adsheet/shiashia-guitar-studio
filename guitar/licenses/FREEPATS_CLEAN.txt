This application uses the 12 sample files mapped by "EGuitarFSBS-clean bridge small 20260807.sfz".
Each FLAC source was converted to mono MP3 at 44.1 kHz / 160 kbps, trimmed to 2.5 seconds,
and faded during its final 0.25 seconds. The converted samples are assets/funk-*.mp3
and are embedded into the clean bank in src/guitar-banks.js for standalone playback.
Original sample note mapping: C2=36, F2=41, A2=45, C3=48, E3=52, G3=55,
B3=59, E4=64, G4=67, B4=71, D5=74, G#5=80.
